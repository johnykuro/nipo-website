# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Guests exploring NIPO on Newcastle Quayside, browsing menus, planning a visit and booking a table from desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present the Japanese-Brazilian restaurant, its food, heritage and hospitality, with clear routes to menus, reservations and visitor information.

## Brand Personality

Refined, warm and atmospheric. Confident, culturally grounded hospitality with concise copy and an image-led editorial presentation.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic steakhouse or buffet cues, tropical carnival imagery, jungle spectacle, novelty Japanese styling and red-heavy compositions. NIPO is a sibling to Rio, not a sub-brand.

## Design Principles

- Express Japanese-Brazilian heritage with care and credibility.
- Let food photography and thoughtful hospitality lead.
- Keep reservations and essential visitor information easy to find.
- Preserve approved logo artwork and the existing brand system.
- Use generous space and restrained interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the WCAG 2.2 AA requirements in DESIGN.md. Keep content keyboard accessible, readable over photography and usable with reduced motion. Essential content must not depend on hover or animation.
