NIPO Brand DNA
Brand name

NIPO

Tagline

Japanese Precision. Brazilian Fire.

Brand essence

NIPO is a premium Japanese-Brazilian restaurant on Newcastle Quayside, rooted in the cultural heritage of the Nipo-Brasileiro community.

The concept brings together Japanese precision, sushi craft, robata-style cooking and ingredient discipline with Brazilian warmth, fire-cooking and generous hospitality.

NIPO should never feel like generic fusion. It is not a theme restaurant, a steakhouse replacement or simply Rio downstairs. It is a refined, atmospheric dining experience built around precision, fire, sushi, craft and cultural depth.

Core positioning

NIPO is a Japanese-Brazilian restaurant where sushi, robata and fire-cooked signatures meet warm Brazilian hospitality.

The brand should sit between the elegance of a premium Japanese restaurant and the warmth of Brazilian fire-cooking. It should feel atmospheric enough for Newcastle Quayside, but refined enough to feel considered, adult and distinctive.

Visual identity

The NIPO logo combines a cherry blossom and central flame.

The blossom represents Japanese elegance, craft and precision.
The flame represents Brazilian fire, warmth and cooking over heat.
The gold wordmark gives the brand its premium restaurant feel.

The current preferred lock-up uses:

Deep green-black background: #15231F
Red blossom mark: #B02028
Gold NIPO wordmark: #F0E098
Ivory flame: #F7F1DE
White or ivory tagline

This is the most commercially clear version of the brand. It gives immediate contrast, holds well on signage and feels premium without becoming overly minimal.

Typography direction

The NIPO wordmark should remain elegant, high-contrast and premium.

Supporting typography should be clean, modern and tightly controlled, with generous letter spacing for taglines, menus and navigation.

Avoid novelty Japanese typefaces, distressed fonts, overly tropical fonts or anything too playful.

Typography should feel closer to a premium hospitality brand than a casual pan-Asian restaurant.

Brand guardrails

NIPO should not be presented as a steakhouse.

NIPO should not be described as generic Brazilian and Japanese fusion.

NIPO should not look or sound like Rio downstairs.

NIPO should feel like a sibling to Rio, not a sub-brand.

NIPO should use the Japanese-Brazilian heritage story with care and credibility.

The colour #15231F should lead the brand world, helping NIPO feel darker, more botanical, more premium and more distinct from Rio.

Any Japanese characters used in the identity, signage or menus should be checked by a fluent Japanese speaker before final production.
