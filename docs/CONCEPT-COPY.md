# NIPO concept copy

Updated 15 September 2026 to match the supplied printed menu introduction, led by “Two cultures. One table.” Home introduces the connection; Concept explains its history, the dining experience and NIPO's purpose of bringing people together through shared food and hospitality.

## Historical source

[JICA: Across the seas — tracing the history of Japanese migration](https://www.jica.go.jp/english/information/topics/2026/p20260723_01.html), published 20 August 2026, checked 15 September 2026. Confirms the 1908 arrival of the Kasato Maru at Santos and Brazil's status as home to the world's largest Nikkei population. Website wording uses “community of Japanese descent outside Japan” to include descendants clearly.

The name's connection to São Paulo and the restaurant's cultural influences come from the owner's supplied menu. NIPO's interpretation and purpose are brand copy, not claims that the current dishes date to 1908.
