11 / Guardrails

NIPO, not spectacle

The system borrows immersive pacing from premium hospitality references while keeping NIPO's own cultural depth, colour discipline, and evening restraint.

Do

  • Lead with nightfall and let ivory create breath.
  • Show sushi craft, robata fire, brass, bamboo, and intimate tables.
  • Use asymmetry, full-bleed imagery, and concise editorial copy.
  • Make every action keyboard accessible and touch friendly.

Do not

  • Use tropical carnival imagery, animated fauna, or bright jungle greens.
  • Present NIPO as a steakhouse, rodizio, or generic fusion restaurant.
  • Use red buttons, red-heavy compositions, or large gold backgrounds.
  • Add novelty Japanese type, glass effects, or decorative card shadows.
